Privacy policy

Task Blocks · last updated 3 November 2026

Task Blocks is a personal task app, made for its owner's own use. It has no server of its own, no user accounts, no adverts and no analytics or tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your own cloud storage, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

Data the app keeps

  • Your tasks (names, dates, notes, categories, checklists) are saved on the device.
  • If you turn on Dropbox sync, your tasks and settings are also saved in your own Dropbox account, which you sign in to yourself. Dropbox's own privacy policy applies to that copy.
  • Sign-in tokens for Dropbox and Google are saved only on the device that signed in. They are never synced.

Google user data (Gmail)

Google sign-in is optional and off until you turn it on. The app asks for one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify, and uses it only for these features:

  • Email to tasks: it reads recent emails from senders you have allowed, and turns those whose subject starts with "Task" (or that have the Gmail label "Task") into tasks. It then changes that email's labels so it is not picked up twice.
  • Starred emails: it lists your starred emails so you can make a task from one or attach it to a task, and optionally removes the star or adds a label afterwards.

The app does not send or delete email. Gmail is read directly between your device and Google. No email data passes through any server run by Task Blocks, because there isn't one.

When an email is attached to a task, the app keeps its subject, sender, date, a short snippet and its Gmail id with that task, on your device and in your Dropbox if sync is on. The full email body and attachments are not stored.

Task Blocks' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the features above, is not used for advertising, is not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and is not transferred to anyone else.

Other device permissions (Android)

  • Calendar (optional): reads upcoming events so you can turn them into tasks.
  • Location (optional): saves where a task was added and powers place reminders. It stays on the device and in your Dropbox.
  • Photos and camera (optional): text in a photo is read on the device to make tasks. The photo is not uploaded.

Removing your data

  • Settings → Google → Sign out deletes the Google token from the device and revokes it at Google. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on that device. The copy in your Dropbox stays until you delete it there.
